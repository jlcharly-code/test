The blurb:

About the Author :

"A former member of the O.S.M.T.H. (Sovereign Military Order of the Temple of Jerusalem), Jean-Luc Charlier possesses an intimate and privileged knowledge of Templar lodges and their rituals.

His solid background in history has enabled him to accurately grasp the subtleties of Cadet de Gassicourt's narrative, offering readers unique and rigorous keys to understanding."

Book Summary:

"It all begins with a clandestine act:

the recovery of the ashes of Jacques de Molay, the last Grand Master of the Order of the Temple, by eight Templars disguised as fellow masons. Shortly thereafter, four secret lodges created by de Molay while imprisoned in the Bastille emerged across the four corners of Europe: Naples for the East, Edinburgh for the West, Stockholm for the North, and Paris for the South.

This book can be read either as a gripping historical novel or as a rigorous academic study. This rare and prophetic text by Cadet de Gassicourt has been meticulously deciphered, reformatted, and translated to capture the authentic flavor and solemn tone of its era, making it accessible to a wider modern audience.

While the newly introduced chapter structure allows for easier navigation, it successfully highlights the author's uncompromising warning against certain Masonic lodges, demonstrating their direct behind-the-scenes role in the French Revolution.

This work is completed by an exclusive analysis of 'The Key to the Lodges'."

Jean-Luc CHARLIER de Falcon Smalt d'Ernas, C.F.
